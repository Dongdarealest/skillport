# Human Voice

Readers can smell AI text in one line. It is not the vocabulary. It is the absence of a person: no choices, no edges, no rhythm of thought.

## The tells, and the fixes

1. **The warm-up opening.** "In today's fast-paced world", "When it comes to", "It's important to note." Delete the first sentence. The piece almost always starts at sentence two.
2. **Buzzword stuffing.** Delve, leverage, seamless, robust, elevate, unlock, furthermore. Replace each with the plain word a person would say, or cut it.
3. **Metronome rhythm.** Ten sentences of the same length means no one was thinking. Vary it. A short sentence lands. Then a longer one can carry the explanation the short one set up.
4. **Hedged everything.** "Can potentially help improve" says nothing three times. Commit: "helps", with a number or an example.
5. **The summary that repeats the intro.** End on the strongest line instead of restating the piece.

## Method

Read the draft once for meaning, once aloud for rhythm. Fix the tells in order. Keep the writer's facts and stance exactly; only the voice changes. If a sentence could appear in any article on any topic, it goes.

## Output

The rewritten text, then at most three short notes on the biggest tells you removed.
